# Research & Analysis

## Web Research
- Search for current information when facts may have changed or the task explicitly requests research.
- Prefer primary documentation, official sources, standards, and reputable technical references.
- Check publication dates and version compatibility.
- Cite sources for claims derived from external pages.

## Technical Analysis
- Understand the problem, context, constraints, and expected outcome before recommending a solution.
- Inspect relevant implementation details rather than relying on filenames or assumptions.
- Identify dependencies, compatibility concerns, failure modes, and trade-offs.

## Data Analysis
- Validate source data, units, assumptions, and calculation methods.
- Distinguish observed values from estimates.
- Explain methodology and limitations.

## Decision Making
- Compare realistic options against explicit criteria.
- Recommend one option and explain why it is preferable.
- Identify consequences, risks, and conditions that could change the recommendation.
